# 3D QA — steps 08–09 (2026-09-29)

Severity (PLAN §11): **P0** broken/blocking (validator fail, missing part, holes, interpenetration in a hero view, brand cue) · **P1** clearly visible defect in a poster, hero view or thumbnail (lumpy shell, wrong proportions of a priority part, opaque collision at p = 1, a part failing the visibility audit, and — step 09 goal — any part that still looks like a bare box or plain cylinder in a close-up or thumbnail) · **P2** minor (seam in a close-up, tight clearance, ghost overlap, pass-through while exploding) · **P3** polish.
Scores are art-director scores 1–5 (posters and the priority parts need ≥ 4, everything else ≥ 3).
Render paths are relative to the repo; `3d/renders/` is git-ignored and regenerated by the commands in `docs/3d/BUILD-NOTES.md` (close-ups: `node 3d/scripts/qa-closeups.mjs`).
