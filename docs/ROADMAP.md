## Goal-step conditions (verbatim from `pipeline/prompts/*.goal.txt`)

### Step 06
All of the following are shown by command output produced after this goal was set: 1) npx convex dev --once completes with no errors; 2) npx tsc --noEmit exits 0; 3) the full Vitest suite including the convex-test files passes with at least 40 tests, among them negative authorization tests; 4) an npx convex run stats query prints non-zero counts for brands, models, generations, engines, categories, products, price tiers and the owner user; 5) docs/DATA-MODEL.md was compared against convex/schema.ts and updated so they match; 6) docs/PROGRESS.md has an entry for steps 05 and 06 and the work is committed with git. Fix whatever blocks any item. Or stop after 30 turns.

### Step 09
The 3D deliverables meet the bar, shown by output produced after this goal was set: 1) node 3d/scripts/validate.mjs prints PASS for public/models/car.glb, confirming the file is at most 8 MB, total triangles are under 800000, every node in public/models/car.manifest.json exists in the GLB, names follow docs/3D-CONTRACT.md, and every 3D category of the contract has at least one node; 2) fresh QA renders made after the last geometry or material change exist for assembled front three-quarter, rear three-quarter, side, top, the full exploded hero view and at least 12 part close-ups, and you opened each image and recorded a PASS verdict with notes in docs/3d/QA.md; 3) docs/3d/QA.md lists no open P0 or P1 visual defects; 4) the hero posters and one thumbnail per category exist under public/images; 5) the work is committed with git. Improve the model wherever a render reveals a defect. Or stop after 25 turns.

### Step 11
The 3D web experience is complete, shown by output produced after this goal was set: 1) npm run build succeeds and npx tsc --noEmit exits 0; 2) Playwright tests for the 3D explorer pass at desktop 1440 and mobile 390 widths: the poster is replaced by live 3D, selecting a part through the accessible parts list and through the test hook opens the part panel listing products from Convex, and a deep link with the deo query parameter opens the right panel; 3) the home page logs no console errors; 4) Lighthouse mobile on the production build of the home page reports LCP under 2.5 seconds, CLS under 0.05, TBT under 300 ms and a performance score of at least 85, with the numbers printed; 5) screenshots of the assembled, exploded and part-selected states at 1440 and 390 were reviewed and verdicts recorded in docs/3d/WEB-QA.md with no open P0 or P1 issues; 6) committed with git. Or stop after 30 turns.

### Step 13
The storefront is complete and verified by output produced after this goal was set: 1) npm run build, npx tsc --noEmit and npm run lint succeed; 2) Playwright e2e passes for OEM search to product, category filtering by vehicle, adding to cart and submitting a guest order with cash on delivery that then exists in Convex with status nova and a domain event, creating a VIN inquiry with a photo upload, and a back-in-stock subscription; 3) an axe scan of home, category, product, cart, checkout and inquiry pages reports zero serious or critical violations; 4) screenshots of every storefront page at 390 and 1440 widths were reviewed and every P0 and P1 issue listed in docs/qa/STOREFRONT.md is fixed; 5) the text-reveal check from the text-reveal skill finds no stuck invisible elements on home, category and product pages; 6) committed with git. Or stop after 35 turns.

### Step 17
Admin panel and Excel import are complete, shown by output produced after this goal was set: 1) npm run build, npx tsc --noEmit and npm run lint succeed; 2) all unit and convex-test suites pass, including import upsert, idempotency, rollback, and staff versus owner authorization; 3) Playwright e2e passes for admin login, creating a product with an image upload, importing the 500-row fixture with auto-mapping and a preview that shows the seeded errors, committing it with correct counts, rolling it back, changing an order from nova to potvrdjena to poslata with a tracking number, answering an inquiry with a quote, and approving a mechanic with a tier; 4) importing the Windows-1250 CSV fixture preserves the Serbian letters c with caron, c with acute, z with caron, s with caron and d with stroke; 5) admin pages were reviewed at 1440 and 1024 widths and all P0 and P1 issues in docs/qa/ADMIN.md are fixed; 6) committed with git.
