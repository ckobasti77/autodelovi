# Design System: Euromotors Plus by OEM Parts

> Portable summary for design tools. The authoritative, complete specification (every token, contrast table, motion, 3D art direction, components, blueprints, microcopy) is **[docs/design/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md](docs/design/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md)**; colour tokens and contrast pairs live in `docs/design/tokens.json`. Recorded from the step-03 concept build (`app/lab/koncept/`); re-run the documenter after step 04 builds the real foundation.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Rendgen — an engineering X-ray at night"**

The car is shown the way a mechanic thinks about it: an assembly of parts, each with a number. The interface is a technical drawing lit at night — a graphite field, warm drawing ink, a cold rim light that only the X-ray car wears, and one safety-orange signal for what is yours and what comes next. The object is the only spectacle; around it the page stays a calm drawing sheet with hairlines, a title block, true dimensions and part codes in a monospace.

Density is comfortable on persuasion surfaces (home, mechanics landing) and dense-but-calm on catalogue, portal and admin. Not a tuning shop, not a generic shop template, not a game HUD.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Graphite night field with warm ink (16:1) and meta that stays ≥ 4.7:1 on every surface.
- Wide uppercase statements (Archivo Expanded), a sturdy UI sans (Public Sans), a monospace only for codes and measurements (Martian Mono).
- Ornament that measures: dimension lines in real millimetres, ruler floor, leader-line callouts, title block with honest facts.
- Squared forms (2 px controls, 4 px cards), hairlines everywhere, no decorative shadows.
- Light „paper“ workspace for staff, with the night sidebar keeping the brand.

## Colors

One accent against cool graphite neutrals; a cyan rim reserved for the X-ray art.

### Primary
- **Safety Orange** (#FF6B1A): primary action (one per view region), selection, focus ring, active stage/tab, the chosen vehicle, „Vaša cena“, unread counts. On light surfaces text uses **#B8470A**.

### Tertiary
- **X-ray Rim** (#9FE9FF, soft #4DB5D6, deep #123642): the car's glass shell and X-ray illustrations only — never UI.

### Neutral
- **Night Field** (#0D0F11): page; **Raised** (#121518) header/footer; **Graphite Card** (#16191D); **Graphite Input** (#1C2025); **Graphite Overlay** (#23282E); **Graphite Hover** (#2B3138).
- **Hairline** (#2C3238), **Hairline Strong** (#434A52), **Control Edge** (#6E767F, ≥ 3:1 on every surface).
- **Drawing Ink** (#F1ECE3), **Secondary Ink** (#C4BEB4), **Meta Ink** (#969088), **Disabled Ink** (#6B6760).
- Signals: success #4FD18B, warning #F4C04A (amber, never orange), danger #FF6166, info #9DB8D0 — always with an icon and a word.
- Paper (admin): field #F3F4F5, panel #FFFFFF, header #F7F8F9, selected row #EEF1F4, ink #121518 / #3D444C / #5D6570.

### Named Rules
**The Course Colour Rule.** Orange marks only the active leg — what is yours or what you do next. It never decorates and never means status.
**The Reserved Rim Rule.** Cyan belongs to the X-ray car. A cyan link, chart or focus ring breaks the world.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo Expanded 700/600 (static instances, self-hosted; Arial fallback)
**Numerals:** Archivo Condensed 600 with tabular figures
**Body Font:** Public Sans (variable)
**Mono:** Martian Mono (dotted zero — 0 and O never confused in OEM numbers)

**Character:** Model-badge capitals for statements over a neutral, government-grade UI sans; an engraved-plate monospace for part numbers.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, clamp 36→56→64 px, 1.0, caps): the hero statement, once per page.
- **Headline** (600, clamp 28→40 px, 1.04, caps): marketing section titles, short category titles.
- **Title** (Public Sans 650, clamp 26→36 px, 1.15, −0.015em): product names, task pages.
- **Body** (400, 16 px, 1.55, 65–72ch).
- **Label** (600, 11–12 px, 0.06em, caps): table headers and badges — never above a heading.
- **Code** (Martian Mono 400, 13 px): OEM/cross numbers, SKU, order numbers, VIN, dimensions.

### Named Rules
**The Mono-Is-Data Rule.** Monospace only for codes and measurements, never for prose or headings.
**The Serbian Type Rule.** č ć ž š đ in every font file; „…“ quotes; no line starts with a dash; money grouped (12.990 RSD), drawing measurements ungrouped (4280).

## Layout

12 columns from 1024 px (8 at 768, 4 below), gutter clamp(16–24 px), margin clamp(16–48 px), content capped at 1440 px with `--pad: max(var(--margin), calc((100% - 1440px) / 2))`. Home hero: copy + search on columns 1–5, the object on 6–12 bottom-aligned with a callout lane above it, stage index full width below; below 1024 px the order is copy → object → vehicle → stage index. Section rhythm clamp(64–128 px). Touch targets ≥ 44 px on mobile.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from surface lightness steps and hairlines; resting UI has no shadows. Overlays commit to one treatment: on night an edge (surface-3 + 1 px #434A52, no shadow); on paper a soft elevation without a hairline (`0 1px 2px rgb(18 21 24 / .10), 0 8px 24px -6px rgb(18 21 24 / .18)`). The sticky header is 88 % opaque with a 12 px blur because the 3D stage moves beneath it.

### Named Rules
**The Edge-And-Light Rule.** Every surface has its edge and a lit top edge; only what floats adds a tight contact shadow — never a hairline plus a wide diffuse shadow, never a glow.

## Shapes

Small soft corners on every box, by role: 4 px on chips and badges, 6 px on buttons and inputs, 8 px on cards, panels and tables, 10 px on pictures, 12 px on dialogs, 14 px on sheet tops; pills only for what is a pill. Nested corners are concentric. Only drawings stay square (corner ticks, dimension lines). 1 px hairlines; 2 px only for focus rings and active indicators. Corner ticks (14 px L-shapes) frame drawing areas.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 6 px.
- **Primary:** orange fill, night-field label (6.74:1), 44 px (hero 56 px attached to the search field).
- **Hover / Focus:** lighter orange on hover, pressed darker + 1 px travel; focus ring 2 px ink on orange, orange elsewhere.
- **Secondary:** transparent with a control-edge border, one per region. **Quiet:** a filled graphite surface for repeated actions (card and row buttons).

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** graphite input fill, 1 px control edge, label always above (13 px), 16 px text.
- **Focus:** orange border + 1 px inner ring. **Error:** danger border, icon + message below.

### Cards
- Graphite card (`panel`), 1 px hairline, 8 px radius, lit top edge, 16 px padding; hover steps the surface up and lifts 1 px; 4:3 render; maker → name (whole-card link) → OEM (mono) → fitment line → stock line → price (condensed, „sa PDV-om“) + quiet action.

### Navigation
- 64 px sticky header: wordmark (the lifted-part mark + name in Archivo Expanded), four text links, „Pozovite“/„Viber“ (from settings), account, cart with an orange count.

### X-ray Callout (signature)
- Orange 1 px leader from the part straight up to an annotation lane, then a shelf; label box graphite-overlay with a 1 px strong hairline, category name, one mechanic note, a live count link with an arrow icon.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep one orange primary action per view region.
- **Do** pair every status colour with an icon and a word.
- **Do** write dimensions from the real model (4280 / 2620 / 1450 mm).
- **Do** use tabular figures for prices, quantities and tables.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use VW blue, Škoda green, SEAT copper or Audi red, or any VAG logo.
- **Don't** add eyebrows/kickers above headings, gradient text, glows on UI, bounce/elastic motion or looping ambient animation.
- **Don't** use cyan outside the X-ray art or monospace outside codes and measurements.
- **Don't** hide copy or content for an entrance — only the site-wide text reveal may.
